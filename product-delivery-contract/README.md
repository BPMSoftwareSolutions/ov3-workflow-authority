# Capability Studio product delivery contract

The unchanged revision-3 [JSON contract](product-delivery-contract.json) and its
[Markdown projection](product-delivery-contract.md) define the complete online
Capability Studio. They contain 22 obligations, 70 criteria, 14 flight definitions,
55 steps, 51 payload schemas and 13 formal properties.

## The delivery agent is OV3

C12.1 requires one objective to drive discovery, planning, supply, correction,
publication, readback and verification without another frontier command.
FP03 assigns orchestration and capability supply to OV3. C13 requires subsequent
maintenance without frontier implementation. C19 governs concrete flights,
failures, compensation and reviewed repairs; C22 governs reusable native supply.

The local orchestration, approval and repair tools formerly stored here did not
fulfill those obligations. They have been removed from the working tree. Do not
recreate them as the route to delivery. Discover and bind existing OV3 capabilities,
including typed System One roles, before proposing missing native supply.

## Reasoning belongs inside the declared process

TypeSafe/System One is the intended bounded inference lane for reuse/compose/new/
unresolved classification, ranking, routing, uncertainty and escalation. General
reasoning handles novel semantics and design through declared roles. Actual
schemas, decision profiles and model execution evidence must be verified; model
names and typed output alone do not establish authority or correctness.

Schema, digest, identity, coverage, clearance, proof and gate decisions remain
native deterministic responsibilities. The illustrative Jev example supplied by
the owner is not a real SDK integration or measured telemetry and is not a local
implementation plan.

## Gates and current state

FP00 prepares and qualifies the verifier toolchain under G-PREPARATION and obtains
an authoritative G-DEFINITION result. Product work then follows the declared
flight dependencies and G-FLIGHT. FP12 verifies the exact final obligation set;
FP13 consumes that receipt and proves review submission and delivery closure.
No future/self receipt is required as its own prerequisite.

Read [verification status](verification-status.md) and the compact
[delivery state](delivery-state.json). Neither authoritative gate has passed.
The preserved [owner authorization](owner-authorization.json) permits conforming
work without another human approval loop. It does not turn a local program into
the native approval process or a model opinion into acceptance.
