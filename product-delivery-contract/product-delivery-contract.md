Contract `capability-studio-product-delivery.v1`, revision **3**. Definition: **PENDING_OV3_VALIDATION**. Product: **NOT_DELIVERED**.

## Definition and final delivery gates

Before the definition gate, only contract authoring, read-only discovery, verification-toolchain preparation, definition validation are permitted. Product implementation and publication require the gate and the specific flight clearance.

### G-PREPARATION

FP00 verifier toolchain only; no product implementation, deployment or business-capability execution.

- Exact retained preparation scope, authored feature/DSL/schema/provider closure and typed input mappings.
- Existing owner authorization and concrete materialized reviewed plan, with applicable clearance under current estate policy.
- Only existing admission authority admits verifier supply; the candidate verifier cannot authorize its own installation.
- Positive accepting fixtures and adversarial negative controls, independent checker review or checked certificate, rollback and hosted readback.
- This gate has no dependency on G-DEFINITION; it permits verifier preparation only. Product flights still require G-DEFINITION and G-FLIGHT.
- Preparation plan uses existing estate review/clearance capabilities. G-PREPARATION names a policy predicate, not an assumed installed capability. Materialize the actual admission bindings before execution.

### G-DEFINITION

Required verdict: `DEFINITION_COMPLETE`. `DefinitionValidationRequest → GateReceipt`. Authoritative capability: `validate-product-delivery-definition`; binding state: **REQUIRED_NOT_YET_VERIFIED**.

- Read and verify exact owner/deck/topology/strategy and contract/schema pins
- Every obligation has objective criteria, typed payloads, evidence and achieving/verifying flights
- Plan/step DAGs and references valid; gaps have bounded owned supply/repair paths
- Authority, refusals, progress and formal methods/assumptions/bounds defined
- Non-vacuous accepting path and independent semantic review; blocking findings reconciled
- Authoritative OV3 verifier reads retained artifacts itself; no caller self-attestation

Required verdict: `OBLIGATIONS_COMPLETE`. `ObligationValidationRequest → GateReceipt`. Authoritative capability: `validate-product-delivery-obligations`; binding state: **REQUIRED_NOT_YET_VERIFIED**.

- Initial and maintenance agency without frontier implementation

## Flight plans

These are concrete delivery-plan definitions, not cleared execution records. Each ordered step names its intended work, input/outcome contract and a candidate primitive or proposed responsibility. Candidate reuse is not a binding claim. Materialization must resolve a compatible capability or declared composition, prove mappings, and record the executable estate plan revision before clearance.

A missing performer is a supply obligation, not permission for a frontier script or fake successful result. Existing authorization is honored; current estate policy determines any reserved exact-digest approval.
